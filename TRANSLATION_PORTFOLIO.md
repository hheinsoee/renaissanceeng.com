# Renaissance Translation Services — Portfolio & History

## About

Renaissance Translation Services has been operating since **2012**, following the involvement of its director, **Aung Aung Naing**, in an international development initiative known as the **Pyoe Pin Programme**.

The Pyoe Pin Programme, launched in 2007 and managed by the British Council Yangon, is a long-term effort to promote inclusive governance, accountability, and sustainable development in Myanmar. It brings together civil society, government, and the private sector to address critical issues such as land reform, education, fisheries, forestry, and health.

Prior to this, Renaissance occasionally provided translation services for NGOs in Rakhine State. After joining the Pyoe Pin Programme, Renaissance became a key provider of translation and interpretation services — including **simultaneous** and **consecutive interpretation** — for partner organizations until 2018.

Since then, Renaissance has expanded its portfolio, delivering services to major international organizations.
